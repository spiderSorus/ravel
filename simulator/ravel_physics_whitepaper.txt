AbstractRavel is a deterministic, procedural multiverse where reality is defined not by static assets, but by Harmonic Chords. Unlike traditional simulations that rely on scripted events, Ravel utilizes a "Physics of Quality" based on the 64 Hexagrams of the I Ching. The universe begins in a primordial, low-resolution state and evolves through specific Epochs of Complexity, unlocking higher-fidelity dimensions of interaction as the collective consciousness expands. This paper outlines the fundamental data structures, the evolutionary mechanics, and the sensor-based architecture of the Ravel Engine.2. The Core Physics: The ChordThe fundamental atom of the Ravel universe is the Chord. It is a vector of 64 integers, representing the entity's affinity to the 64 Gates of the I Ching.2.1 The GateEach Gate represents a specific archetype of reality (e.g., Gate 1: Creative Power, Gate 2: Receptive Flow).Positive Value (+): Active expression (Yang). The entity generates this quality.Negative Value (-): Receptive void (Yin). The entity consumes or requires this quality.Zero (0): Dormancy. The entity is blind to this aspect of reality.2.2 The Taijitu (The Binary Base)Before a Chord is assigned, every entity exists as a binary state, determining its fundamental interaction model:Object (Yin/Noun): Occupies space, collides, has mass. (e.g., Plants, Walls).Action (Yang/Verb): Overlays space, mutates, drives change. (e.g., Hunger, Spells).3. Evolutionary Cosmology: The EpochsThe universe is not static; it is Resolution Gated.
