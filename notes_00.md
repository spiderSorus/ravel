# RAVEL: Conceptual Architecture
**Philosophy, Physics, and Topology**

---

## I. The Core Philosophy: "To Be is To Resonate"

* **The Universe as Sensor:** The universe is not a "Controller" or "God" that dictates events. It is a **Passive Listener** that samples the aggregate noise of all things to determine the current "Quality" of reality.
* **The Chord:** The fundamental atom of existence is not matter, but frequency. Every entity is defined by a **Chord**—a vector of 64 integers representing harmonic affinity to the 64 Gates of the I Ching.
* **Homeostasis:** The universe is a deterministic state machine seeking balance (Zero). It is perfectly still unless acted upon by an imbalance.

---

## II. The Object Model: The Ontological Collapse

* **Everything is One:** We removed the distinction between "Player," "Item," "Action," and "Planet." Everything is a `RavelObject`.
* **The Taijitu (Binary Base):** Every object exists in one of two fundamental states:
    * **Yin (Object/Noun):** Physical. It occupies space, has mass, and blocks movement.
    * **Yang (Action/Verb):** Spiritual. It overlays space, passes through objects, and causes mutation.
* **Resolution Gating:** Complexity is not free. Objects have a "Resolution" ($R$) that limits their bandwidth. An object with $R=1$ is literally blind to the existence of Gate 64. Evolution is the process of unlocking higher resolution.

---

## III. The Epistemology: "To See is To Be"

* **All Objects are Lenses:** We rejected the idea of a separate "Camera" or "View." Perception is defined by **Composition**.
    * A "Sword" is an object that grants the resolution to perceive "Conflict."
    * A "Microscope" is an object that grants the resolution to perceive "Bacteria."
* **The Aggregate View:** A Player does not "have" a perspective. The Player *is* a collection of objects. Their view of reality is the sum of the Lenses they are holding.
* **The Overlay:** Reality is filtered. If your current composition lacks the Lens for Gate 30 (Fire), you are literally blind to heat. It renders as "Void" or "Fog."

---

## IV. The Topology: Relational Space

* **The Death of Coordinates:** We discarded Cartesian coordinates ($x, y$) as a primary truth.
* **Containment vs. Location:** "Where am I?" is a question of **Hierarchy**, not Geometry.
    * *Truth:* The Bacteria is in the Fluid. The Fluid is in the Beaker.
    * *Illusion:* "Latitude/Longitude" is an **Overlay**. It is a data service provided by a high-resolution "Planet Object." A low-resolution entity (Bacteria) cannot access this service and thus has no concept of geography.
* **Navigation by Gradient:** Since there are no coordinates, movement is driven by **Harmonic Attraction**. Entities "smell" their way through the graph, migrating from one Container to another based on resonance (e.g., a "Thirsty" object naturally migrates toward a "Water" container).

---

## V. The Ecology: Supply & Demand

* **The Vacuum:** The Universe does not spawn monsters randomly. It spawns them to fix a problem.
* **The Loop:**
    1.  The Universe detects a massive spike in **Fire (+Yang)**.
    2.  This creates a vacuum (Demand) for **Water (-Yin)**.
    3.  The Spawner responds by generating "Ice Spirits."
    4.  Players/Ecology consume the Ice Spirits, neutralizing the Fire.
    5.  The Universe returns to balance.

---

## VI. Visualization: The Cosmic Radar

* **The Rave Mandala:** The visual representation of this data is the 64-spoke wheel of the I Ching.
* **Geometric Opposition:** The wheel is arranged so that opposing forces (Gate 1 Creative vs. Gate 2 Receptive) are geometrically opposite (180°), creating a visual "Tensor" of the universe's state.
